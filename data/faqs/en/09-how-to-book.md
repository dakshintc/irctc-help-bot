## How do I book a train ticket on IRCTC? How to book a ticket online?

- Log in at irctc.co.in and open "Plan My Journey" (Plan my travel).
- Select the From and To stations, the journey date, the class and the quota, then click "Find Trains".
- Select the class shown under the train and click "Book Now".
- Enter passenger name, age, gender and berth preference, then continue to payment.
- Choose a payment option and click "Make Payment"; after success IRCTC shows the ticket confirmation and a "Print ERS" button.
- The Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS) is the e-ticket. Carry a printed or SMS/PNR copy plus a valid photo ID.
- Internet booking and cancellation hours are 00:20 hrs to 23:45 hrs.

## How do I register or create a new IRCTC account? How to sign up?

- Individual registration on IRCTC is free.
- A valid mobile number and a valid email ID are mandatory to register.
- After sign-up, IRCTC emails the user ID, password and an activation link, and sends a mobile verification code by SMS.
- Activate the account with the email activation link plus the mobile verification code.
- The password can be changed later from the "Change Password" option after login.
