## Keep your account safe

- Use only the official IRCTC website https://www.irctc.co.in or the official IRCTC Rail Connect app.
- IRCTC never asks for your password, OTP, card PIN, CVV or UPI PIN over chat, phone or email.
- Never share your IRCTC user ID, password or OTP with a friend, agent, caller or chatbot.
- Do not enter your IRCTC login on links received by SMS, WhatsApp or email; type the address yourself.
- Log out after booking on a shared or public computer, and change your password if you suspect misuse.

## Safe payment habits

- Pay only on the payment page that opens from the official IRCTC site or app.
- Check the padlock and the domain before entering card or net-banking details.
- IRCTC refunds go back to the original payment account; nobody should ask you to install a remote-access app or share a screen to process a refund.
- If money is debited but no ticket is issued, do not pay again immediately; note the transaction reference and contact IRCTC customer care.

## What this assistant will never ask for

- This assistant never asks for your IRCTC password, OTP, card number, CVV, UPI ID, UPI PIN or Aadhaar number.
- If you paste any of those, the message is rejected and not processed or stored.
- This assistant cannot log in to IRCTC, book, cancel, or check live PNR, seat or fare data on your behalf.

## Official help

- IRCTC customer care: dial 139 (Indian Railways enquiry) or use the contact options on https://www.irctc.co.in.
- Refund and cancellation rules: https://www.irctc.co.in/nget/refundrules
- For a failed transaction, keep the PNR, transaction ID, amount, date and bank details ready before contacting support.
