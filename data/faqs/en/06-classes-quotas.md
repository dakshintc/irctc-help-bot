## Class codes

- 1A — AC First Class: air-conditioned, most expensive, private cabins, includes meals on many trains.
- 2A — AC 2 Tier: air-conditioned sleeper with curtains, 4 berths per bay and 2 side berths.
- 3A — AC 3 Tier: air-conditioned sleeper with 6 berths per bay and 2 side berths; most common AC class.
- 3E — AC 3 Economy: a lower-fare version of AC 3 Tier used on some trains.
- CC — AC Chair Car: air-conditioned seating, usually on day trains such as Shatabdi.
- EC — Executive Chair Car: premium air-conditioned seating, usually on premium day trains.
- SL — Sleeper Class: non-AC sleeping coach with 6 berths per bay and 2 side berths; cheapest reserved sleeper.
- 2S — Second Sitting: non-AC reserved bench seating; cheapest reserved class.
- FC — First Class: non-AC first class, available only on some trains.

## Quota codes

- GN — General Quota: open quota for all passengers.
- LD — Ladies Quota: reserved for female passengers.
- TQ — Tatkal Quota: last-minute booking, opens one day before journey.
- PT — Premium Tatkal Quota: dynamic-fare Tatkal.
- SS — Female above 45 years / Senior Citizen travelling alone.
- HP — Physically Handicapped (Divyaang) Quota.
- FT — Foreign Tourist Quota.
- DF — Defence Quota; DP — Duty Pass Quota; RE — Railway Employee on duty.
- YU — Yuva Quota; LB — Lower Berth Quota.
- HQ — Head Quarters / high official Quota; PH — Parliament House Quota.
- OS — Out Station Quota; RS — Road Side Quota; GNRS — General Quota Road Side.
- PQ — Pooled Quota; RAC — Reservation Against Cancellation (a berth-sharing provision, not a separate quota).

## Quota rules to remember

- Tatkal quota cannot be combined with Ladies or General quota.
- Tatkal quota is not available in every train, class or station pair; check availability first.
- A quota only controls how many seats are released for that group — it does not change the class or fare rules.
- Choose the quota on the "Plan My Journey" page before clicking Find Trains.
