## How many tickets can I book in a month on IRCTC? What is the monthly booking limit?

- A user ID that is not Aadhaar linked can book a maximum of 12 tickets in a month.
- A user ID that is Aadhaar linked can book a maximum of 24 tickets in a month.
- For the 24-ticket limit, at least one passenger on the ticket must also be verifiable through Aadhaar.
- Earlier the limits were 6 and 12 tickets. Limits can change, so check irctc.co.in for the current limit.

## How do I link or verify Aadhaar on IRCTC to book more tickets?

- Log in at irctc.co.in and open "Link Your Aadhaar" under the "My Account" menu.
- Enter your name as per Aadhaar and your Aadhaar number or Virtual ID, tick the declaration and click "Send OTP".
- Enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-registered mobile number to finish the KYC.
- To verify a passenger, open "Add/Modify Master List" under "My Profile" and add the passenger with the Aadhaar number.
- Never share your OTP with anyone other than on the official Aadhaar page of IRCTC.
