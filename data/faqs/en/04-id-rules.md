## ID proof accepted during the journey

- ID details are not required to be entered while booking an e-ticket.
- At least one passenger on the PNR must carry one of the accepted photo identity cards in original during the journey.
- Accepted IDs: Passport.
- Accepted IDs: Voter photo identity card issued by the Election Commission of India.
- Accepted IDs: Driving Licence issued by an RTO.
- Accepted IDs: PAN card issued by the Income Tax Department.
- Accepted IDs: Photo identity card with a serial number issued by a Central or State Government.
- Accepted IDs: Student identity card with photograph issued by a recognised school or college.
- Accepted IDs: Nationalised bank passbook with photograph.
- Accepted IDs: Credit card issued by a bank with a laminated photograph.
- Accepted IDs: Aadhaar, m-Aadhaar or e-Aadhaar.
- Accepted IDs: Photo identity card with a serial number issued by a public sector undertaking, district administration, municipal body or panchayat administration.
- Aadhaar or Driving Licence shown from the DigiLocker "Issued Documents" section is accepted; documents in the "Uploaded Documents" section are not accepted.

## If ID is missing

- If no passenger on the e-ticket can show an accepted original photo ID during the journey, the passenger is treated as travelling without a ticket.
- The case is then dealt with as per extant Railway Rules, which may include a penalty.
- A photocopy, scanned image on the phone, or someone else's ID is not a substitute for the original document (DigiLocker issued documents are the exception).

## Tatkal and other cases

- For Tatkal e-tickets, ID details are not entered at booking, but an original accepted photo ID must be carried during travel.
- The accommodation booked on an e-ticket is not transferable and is valid only for the passengers named on the ticket.
