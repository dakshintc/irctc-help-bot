## How to cancel an e-ticket

- E-tickets can be cancelled only through the IRCTC e-ticketing website or the IRCTC Rail Connect app.
- Log in and go to My Account → My Transactions → Booked Ticket History.
- Select the ticket, choose the passengers to cancel and confirm the cancellation.
- Cancellation is confirmed online and the refund is credited back to the account or payment method used for booking.
- For partial cancellation, print the modified Electronic Reservation Slip separately.
- Refund is processed as per extant Railway Refund Rules; bank/payment-gateway charges and IRCTC service charges are not refunded.

## Cancellation charges for confirmed tickets

These are the flat charges per passenger when a confirmed ticket is cancelled more than 48 hours before scheduled departure:

- AC First / Executive Class: ₹240 plus GST.
- First Class / AC 2 Tier: ₹200 plus GST.
- AC Chair Car / AC 3 Tier / AC 3 Economy: ₹180 plus GST.
- Sleeper Class: ₹120.
- Second Class: ₹60.

For later cancellations the charge is a percentage of the fare, subject to the flat minimum above:

- Between 48 hours and up to 12 hours before departure: 25% of the fare plus GST for AC classes.
- Between 12 hours and up to 4 hours before departure: 50% of the fare plus GST for AC classes.
- No refund on a confirmed ticket if it is not cancelled, or TDR is not filed online, up to 4 hours before scheduled departure.

## RAC, waitlisted and partial tickets

- An RAC or waitlisted ticket gets a refund after deducting clerkage of ₹60 per passenger plus GST, if cancelled up to 30 minutes before scheduled departure.
- No refund is given on an RAC or waitlisted ticket after 30 minutes before scheduled departure.
- If a waitlisted or RAC passenger is confirmed before the final reservation chart, the ticket is treated as reserved and normal cancellation charges apply.
- On a partly confirmed ticket, confirmed passengers can get a full refund less clerkage if the whole ticket is cancelled up to 30 minutes before departure.

## Train cancelled or running very late

- If IRCTC/Indian Railways cancels the train, confirmed and RAC e-tickets get an automatic full refund to the original payment account. No cancellation or TDR is needed.
- If the train is running more than 3 hours late and the passenger does not travel, no cancellation charge or clerkage is deducted, provided the e-ticket TDR is filed online before the train's actual departure.

## TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt)

- TDR is the online refund request used when a ticket cannot be cancelled normally, for example train cancelled, train late, diversion, or travel not undertaken.
- For e-tickets, TDR can be filed online up to 72 hours after the scheduled departure of the train from the boarding station.
- After the reservation chart is prepared, a cancellation claim for an e-ticket is sent by email to etickets@irctc.co.in with full ticket details; IRCTC processes it with the Railway administration.

## Where to cancel or check refund

- Cancel online: https://www.irctc.co.in → My Account → My Transactions → Booked Ticket History.
- Refund rules: https://www.irctc.co.in/nget/refundrules
- If money was debited but no ticket was issued, IRCTC refunds the full fare and convenience fee, but bank/card transaction charges may be forfeited.
