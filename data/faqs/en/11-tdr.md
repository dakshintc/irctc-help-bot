## Can I cancel my ticket after the chart is prepared?

- Until the chart is prepared, an e-ticket can be cancelled online and the refund follows Railway refund rules.
- After the chart is prepared, you must file an online TDR to ask for a refund.
- No refund is given on a confirmed ticket that is not cancelled, or for which TDR is not filed online, up to 4 hours before scheduled departure.
- The refund decision is taken by the Railways, not by IRCTC.

## What is a TDR? What does TDR mean?

- TDR means Ticket Deposit Receipt.
- It is a refund claim for a ticket you could not use, or could only use in part.
- Only the user who booked the ticket can file the TDR.
- Filing a TDR does not guarantee a refund. The concerned Zonal Railway accepts or rejects it as per Railway refund rules.

## How do I file a TDR online?

- Log in at irctc.co.in.
- Go to "My Account", then "My Transactions", then "File TDR".
- Choose the PNR for which you want to file the TDR.
- Select the reason and submit the claim.
- A TDR cannot be edited after submission, so check the details before you submit.
- For e-tickets, TDR can be filed online up to 72 hours after the scheduled departure of the train from the boarding station.
- File on time. A claim filed after the deadline is not accepted.

## How do I file a TDR if the train is cancelled?

- If IRCTC/Indian Railways cancels the train, confirmed and RAC e-tickets get an automatic full refund to the original payment account. No cancellation or TDR is needed.
- If the train is running more than 3 hours late and you do not travel, no cancellation charge is deducted, provided the e-ticket TDR is filed online before the train's actual departure.
- For other cases, TDR can be filed online up to 72 hours after the scheduled departure of the train from the boarding station.

## When can I file a TDR? Train late or AC not working?

- Typical reasons include a train running very late, the AC not working, a route change, or a coach not attached.
- Each reason has its own deadline and rules, set by Indian Railways.
- Filing a false claim can lead to action, including cancellation of your IRCTC ID.

## How long does a TDR refund take?

- The refund is processed as per Railway rules and can take weeks or even two months or more.
- If approved, the refund is credited to the same account used for booking.
- Check the TDR status in "My Transactions" on irctc.co.in.
