## What a PNR is

- PNR (Passenger Name Record) is a 10-digit number printed on the e-ticket or Electronic Reservation Slip.
- It is the reference number for the whole booking: journey, train, class, passengers, fare and current status.
- One PNR can contain one or more passengers; the status can differ for each passenger.

## Where to check PNR status

- IRCTC website: open https://www.irctc.co.in, click "PNR STATUS" in the top menu, enter the 10-digit PNR and the captcha.
- IRCTC Rail Connect app: log in and use the PNR enquiry option.
- Indian Railways enquiry: https://www.indianrail.gov.in → PNR Enquiry, or the NTES app.
- SMS: send the 10-digit PNR to 139 from an Indian mobile number.
- The result shows the train, journey date, class, coach and berth (if allotted) and the current booking status.

## Status words you may see

- CNF / Confirmed: berth is confirmed.
- RAC: Reservation Against Cancellation — you can travel, but the berth may be shared until it is confirmed.
- WL: waitlisted — travel is allowed only if the ticket is confirmed or becomes RAC before the final chart.
- GNWL, RLWL, PQWL, TQWL: waitlist types by quota (general, remote location, pooled, Tatkal).
- The reservation chart is prepared a few hours before departure; the final status is visible after chart preparation.

## What this assistant cannot do

- This assistant cannot look up a live PNR, seat availability, fare, coach position or running status.
- Never share your IRCTC password, OTP or payment details with anyone, including a chatbot, to "check PNR".
- Always verify the final status on irctc.co.in or the Rail Connect app before travelling.
