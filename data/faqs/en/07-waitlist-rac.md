## Waitlisted e-tickets

- IRCTC allows waitlisted e-tickets to be booked on all trains.
- A waitlisted ticket is not a confirmed berth. Travel is allowed only if the passenger is confirmed or RAC after the reservation chart is prepared.
- If all passengers on a PNR are still waitlisted after chart preparation, their names are dropped from the chart.
- The fare for a fully waitlisted PNR is credited back to the original payment account after deducting clerkage.

## Cancelling a waitlisted ticket

- A waitlisted or RAC e-ticket can be cancelled online up to 30 minutes before the scheduled departure of the train.
- After that, no refund is admissible.
- Cancellation through the internet is permitted only before the reservation chart is prepared.
- For claims after chart preparation, email etickets@irctc.co.in with the full ticket details; IRCTC processes the claim with the Railway administration and credits the sanctioned refund.

## RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)

- RAC lets you board and travel even though the berth is not fully confirmed.
- An RAC berth can be shared with another RAC passenger until it is confirmed.
- If an RAC ticket is confirmed before the final chart, it is treated as a reserved ticket for cancellation charges.
- If you do not travel on an RAC ticket, cancel it at least 30 minutes before departure to get the fare back after clerkage.

## Partly confirmed tickets

- On a party or family e-ticket, some passengers may be confirmed while others are waitlisted or RAC.
- If the whole ticket is cancelled up to 30 minutes before departure, confirmed passengers can get a full refund less clerkage.
- If only some passengers do not travel, a certificate from the ticket checking staff is needed and a TDR must be filed online within 72 hours of the train's actual arrival at the destination.
