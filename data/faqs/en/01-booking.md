## Registration and login

- Individual registration on IRCTC is free.
- A valid mobile number and a valid email ID are mandatory to register.
- After sign-up, IRCTC emails the user ID, password and an activation link, and sends a mobile verification code by SMS.
- The account is activated with the email activation link plus the mobile verification code.
- The password can be changed later from the "Change Password" option after login.

## Steps to book an e-ticket

- Log in at irctc.co.in and open "Plan My Journey" (Plan my travel).
- Select the From and To stations, the journey date, the class and the quota, then click "Find Trains".
- Select the class shown under the train and click "Book Now".
- Enter passenger name, age, gender and berth preference, then continue to payment.
- Choose a payment option and click "Make Payment"; after success IRCTC shows the ticket confirmation and a "Print ERS" button.
- The Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS) is the e-ticket. Carry a printed or SMS/PNR copy plus a valid photo ID.

## E-ticket vs I-ticket

- An e-ticket is booked online, paid online and the ERS is printed by the passenger.
- An I-ticket is also booked online but a paper ticket is printed and delivered by post/courier.
- Booking and cancellation hours for internet tickets are 00:20 hrs to 23:45 hrs.

## Payment options

- IRCTC groups payment choices under credit cards, debit cards, net banking, wallets and multiple payment services.
- Select the option, click "Make Payment" and complete the transaction on the bank or payment page.
- IRCTC does not need your card PIN, CVV, OTP or UPI PIN in chat. Never share those with anyone.

## Changes, concession and break journey

- To postpone or prepone a journey online, cancel the original ticket and book a fresh ticket.
- Partial cancellation is allowed: go to My Account → My Transactions → Booked Ticket History, select the ticket and cancel only the passengers you want.
- Break-journey booking is not available for internet e-tickets.
- Student concession is not available on e-tickets; Divyaang passengers with a Railway-issued photo identity card can avail the concession.
- A confirmed e-ticket is valid only when at least one passenger on the PNR shows an accepted photo ID in original during the journey.
