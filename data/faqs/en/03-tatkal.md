## Tatkal timing and advance reservation

- Tatkal advance reservation period is one day, excluding the date of journey from the train's originating station.
- Example: for a train leaving the origin on the 2nd, Tatkal booking opens on the 1st.
- Tatkal opens at 10:00 hrs for AC classes (1A / 2A / 3A / CC / EC / 3E).
- Tatkal opens at 11:00 hrs for non-AC classes (SL / FC / 2S).
- Booking is from the train's originating station and the quota must be selected as TATKAL on the booking page.

## Classes and quota rules

- IRCTC's Tatkal FAQ says Tatkal booking is allowed in all classes except First AC and Executive Class.
- Ladies quota and General quota cannot be selected together with Tatkal quota.
- For some trains, classes or locations Tatkal quota may not be available; check availability before booking.
- No concession is allowed in Tatkal booking, and senior citizen concession is not allowed in Tatkal quota.
- A maximum of 4 passengers can be booked per PNR on a Tatkal e-ticket.

## Tatkal charges

Tatkal charge is extra, over and above the normal fare, per passenger:

- Second Sitting (2S): 10% of basic fare, minimum ₹10, maximum ₹15.
- Sleeper (SL): 30% of basic fare, minimum ₹100, maximum ₹200.
- AC Chair Car (CC): 30% of basic fare, minimum ₹125, maximum ₹225.
- AC 3 Tier (3A): 30% of basic fare, minimum ₹300, maximum ₹400.
- AC 2 Tier (2A): 30% of basic fare, minimum ₹400, maximum ₹500.
- Executive Class (EC): 30% of basic fare, minimum ₹400, maximum ₹500.
- The same Tatkal charges apply in peak and non-peak periods.

## Booking and travel rules

- Select the Tatkal check box on the "Plan my travel" page along with the other journey details.
- Entry of ID card details is not required while booking a Tatkal e-ticket, but one passenger must carry an accepted photo ID in original during the journey.
- Tatkal accommodation is not transferable and is valid only for the passengers named on the ticket.
- Tatkal tickets can be cancelled as per the normal Railway refund rules; Tatkal charges are not refunded separately.
- Tatkal e-ticket booking can be done only through the official IRCTC website or Rail Connect app.
