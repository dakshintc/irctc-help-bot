## How do I postpone or prepone my journey? Can I change the travel date?

- Online, the journey date cannot be changed on the same ticket.
- Cancel the original ticket and book a fresh ticket for the new date.
- Cancellation charges apply as per Railway refund rules.
- Partial cancellation is allowed. Go to My Account, then My Transactions, then Booked Ticket History, select the ticket and cancel only the passengers you want.

## What if I forget to carry my photo ID? Is ID proof compulsory for an e-ticket?

- One passenger on the PNR must show a valid photo ID in original during the journey.
- Without it, the passengers are treated as travelling without a ticket and handled as per Railway rules.
- Valid IDs include Passport, Voter ID, Driving Licence, PAN card, Aadhaar, m-Aadhaar and e-Aadhaar.
- A government or PSU photo ID with a serial number, a student ID with photo and a nationalised bank passbook with photo are also accepted.
- Aadhaar or Driving Licence shown from the "Issued Documents" section of DigiLocker is accepted. Documents you uploaded yourself are not accepted.
